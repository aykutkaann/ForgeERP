# ForgeERP Glossary (Ubiquitous Language)

## How to read this
Each term is the exact word used in code, APIs, and conversations.
No synonyms — if the glossary says "Item", nobody says "Product" or "Material".

Item — the thing the business tracks (raw material, component, finished good)
Bill of Materials (BOM) — the recipe: what parts make up a product
BOM Line — a single row in that recipe (which item, how many)
Routing — the sequence of steps to produce an item
Operation — a single step within a routing
