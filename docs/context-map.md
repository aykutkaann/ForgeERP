# ForgeERP Context Map

## Relationships

### Catalog → Inventory
- Direction: Catalog is upstream, Inventory is downstream
- What flows: Item definitions, item codes, unit of measure (UOM), item type
- How: Inventory reads item master data from Catalog to track stock correctly

---

### Catalog → Sales
- Direction: Catalog is upstream, Sales is downstream
- What flows: Sellable item information, item codes, product descriptions
- How: Sales uses Catalog data when creating customer orders

---

### Catalog → Manufacturing
- Direction: Catalog is upstream, Manufacturing is downstream
- What flows: Manufactured item definitions, raw material definitions
- How: Manufacturing uses Catalog items inside BOMs and production orders

---

### Catalog → Planning
- Direction: Catalog is upstream, Planning is downstream
- What flows: Item master data, item types, planning-related item attributes
- How: Planning reads Catalog data during MRP calculations

---

### Inventory → Sales
- Direction: Inventory is upstream, Sales is downstream
- What flows: Available stock quantities, reserved stock information
- How: Sales checks Inventory before confirming customer orders

---

### Sales → Planning
- Direction: Sales is upstream, Planning is downstream
- What flows: Sales orders, demand forecasts, required quantities
- How: Planning uses customer demand to calculate procurement and production needs

---

### Manufacturing → Inventory
- Direction: Manufacturing is upstream, Inventory is downstream
- What flows: Finished goods receipts, material consumption transactions
- How: Manufacturing updates Inventory when materials are consumed or products are completed

---

### Inventory → Manufacturing
- Direction: Inventory is upstream, Manufacturing is downstream
- What flows: Raw material availability, stock reservations
- How: Manufacturing reserves and consumes materials from Inventory for production orders

---

### Manufacturing → Planning
- Direction: Manufacturing is upstream, Planning is downstream
- What flows: BOM structures, routing data, production capacity information
- How: Planning uses manufacturing data during MRP and production planning

---

### Planning → Manufacturing
- Direction: Planning is upstream, Manufacturing is downstream
- What flows: Planned production orders, manufacturing schedules
- How: Manufacturing executes planned work generated by Planning

---

### Inventory → Planning
- Direction: Inventory is upstream, Planning is downstream
- What flows: Current stock levels, safety stock, inventory shortages
- How: Planning uses inventory balances to determine replenishment needs



---

## Overall Flow

1. Catalog defines all items used across the system
2. Sales creates customer demand
3. Inventory tracks stock availability
4. Planning calculates what needs to be purchased or manufactured
5. Manufacturing produces finished goods and consumes materials
6. Inventory is updated with completed products
